For my senior design project at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, I worked on a five-person interdisciplinary team to build a minimum viable product of an app that helps people living with dementia manage daily life. FRED, short for Friendly Robot to Ease Dementia, began as a social robot developed by our sponsor, Dr. Xiaopeng Zhao; the app set out to bring its help to a phone.

The app is built with Flutter, with user accounts managed through Firebase. It includes:

- a friendly conversational chatbot built on ChatGPT, which you can type or talk to;
- reminders and a task planner;
- cognitively stimulating games;
- voice controls.

We also designed a caregiver side, so that future versions can connect a person living with dementia with the people who care for them. Our final design report describes the app, how we tested it and the next steps for its development.
