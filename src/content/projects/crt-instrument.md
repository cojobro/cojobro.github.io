I made a musical instrument out of an old CRT television. It makes its sound from the controlled oscillations of the electric field that the cathode-ray tube produces.

Soft potentiometers along the wooden neck work like frets. An Arduino reads where I press and changes the frequency of the tube's electric field to play different notes. To hear it, all you need to do is hold the end of an aux cable, plugged into an amplifier, near the screen.

It's one of my favorite things I've built.
